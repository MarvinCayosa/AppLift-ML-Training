EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report presents a comprehensive evaluation of a Support Vector Machine classifier
for exercise form quality assessment. The model achieved 94.1% test accuracy
with 92.1% +/- 1.8% cross-validation accuracy.
Dataset dimensionality was reduced from 258 to 60 features (76.7% reduction).
The classifier discriminates between 3 movement quality classes: Clean, Uncontrolled Movement, Abrupt Initiation.
The model uses 328 support vectors (175.4% of training data).
