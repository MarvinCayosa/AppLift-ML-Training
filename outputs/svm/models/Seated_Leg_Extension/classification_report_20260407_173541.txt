EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This report presents a comprehensive evaluation of a Support Vector Machine classifier
for exercise form quality assessment. The model achieved 78.8% test accuracy
with 85.5% +/- 3.1% cross-validation accuracy.
Dataset dimensionality was reduced from 258 to 60 features (76.7% reduction).
The classifier discriminates between 3 movement quality classes: Clean, Pulling Too Fast, Releasing Too Fast.
The model uses 241 support vectors (283.5% of training data).
